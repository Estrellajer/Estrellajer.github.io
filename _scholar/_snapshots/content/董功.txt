My research focuses on building self-improving AI systems that can learn continuously, adapt reliably, and scale efficiently in open-ended and non-stationary environments. Recent main research topics include:
Continual & Lifelong Learning
• Forgetting mitigation
• Agentic & online learning
• Continual learning LLMs & MLLMs
Foundation Model Training
• Post-training
• Test-time learning
• Efficient alignment and fine-tuning
Generative Models
• Image & video generation
• Diffusion models
• Control & alignment
Adaptive & Modular Learning
• Memory model & mechanism
• (Dynamic) mixture-of-experts
Vision & Multimodal Learning
• Semantic and depth perception
• Image restoration & enhancement
