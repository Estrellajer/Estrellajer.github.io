I am a graduate student (since 2025) at the **School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications**, advised by **[Prof. Fang Quan](https://quanfang.github.io/)** in the **MKC Group**. I am also an Intern at **ByteDance Data**, mentored by **[He Junwei](https://jweihe.github.io/)**.

My research centers on **Post-Training** and **Continual Learning** of LLMs.

It has been about <span class="days-since" data-since="2022-11-30">1,450</span> days since ChatGPT came out, so why hasn't the world changed yet? Everything I do is part of the search for that answer. Right now that means two questions: how to keep RL rewards from being gamed, since rubric-based rewards can score higher while answers get worse ([ProRubric](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38847)); and how a model can keep learning without forgetting ([CoDe-LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08312), EMNLP 2026).

I'm always happy to collaborate on benchmarks, evaluation, or anything else that moves us closer to AGI. [Get in touch](mailto:liumaoqi@bupt.edu.cn).
