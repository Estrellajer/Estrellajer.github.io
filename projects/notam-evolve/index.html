NOTAM-Evolve

A knowledge-guided self-evolving framework with LLMs for NOTAM interpretation.

research GitHub

NOTAM-Evolve explores how large language models can improve structured interpretation of aviation NOTAMs through a closed-loop process that combines reasoning, retrieval, and iterative refinement.

This page is intentionally concise: enough context to understand the problem, the contribution, and where to find the code, without falling back to the original template’s demo-heavy style.
