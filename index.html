I am a graduate student (since 2025) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, advised by Prof. Fang Quan in the MKC Group. I am also an Intern at ByteDance Data, mentored by He Junwei.

My research centers on Post-Training and Continual Learning of LLMs.

It has been about 1,450 days since ChatGPT came out, so why hasn’t the world changed yet? Everything I do is part of the search for that answer. Right now I care about two things: giving models many more tasks to learn from, with the right environments to practice in; and making as many of those tasks verifiable as possible, because anything that can be evaluated can be optimized.

I’m always happy to collaborate on benchmarks, evaluation, or anything else that moves us closer to AGI. Get in touch.
